America's Thrift Stores (Northgate)

Thrift store 248 Northgate Mall Dr #122, Chattanooga, TN 37415

Hours: Mon-Thu 8-9 · Fri-Sat 8-10 · Sun 10-8

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay, checks

Large store with plenty of finds, though several reviewers have run into trouble with the store-credit-only return policy.

💛 Proceeds benefit primarily Adult & Teen Challenge MidSouth, a faith-based addiction recovery program, alongside other local nonprofit partners focused on children's health and job readiness. The chain also reports keeping over 60 million pounds of donated goods out of landfills each year.

Bethel Thrift Store

Thrift store 5135 Hixson Pike, Hixson, TN 37343

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun Closed

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

Known for a strong book and lamp selection, with reviewers appreciating fair pricing and respectful staff.

💛 Proceeds benefit Bethel Bible Village, a children's home for kids who are victims of abuse and neglect, helping cover the cost of providing them a safe, stable home.
