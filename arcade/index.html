Arcade

Chattanooga-themed games from the Thrift Trail. Pick one, play, and get your initials on the high score board.

423 Thrift Rush

A retro thrift-shop dash. Find all 12 items on your list, rush them to the register, and steer clear of the junk. Five levels, and each one is harder than the last.

Arrow keys, swipe or on-screen buttons. Works on phones.

Thrift Trail Rally

Race a loop of real Chattanooga streets, with the real street names on the signs, against the Red, Blue and Green cars. Cross the river on the real bridges, and watch for a little air on the ramps. You start with 60 seconds, and every thrift shop you reach adds time to your clock. Watch for two-way traffic, dogs and grannies at the crosswalks, and grab the speed boosts.

Keys: arrows or W A S D. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
