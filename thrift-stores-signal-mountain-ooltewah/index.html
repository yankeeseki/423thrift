Bachman Bargain Barn

Thrift store Anderson Pike, Signal Mountain, TN 37377

Hours: Sun-Thu Closed · Fri/Sat 9-2

Price: $$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, debit cards

Small weekend thrift shop on Anderson Pike on Signal Mountain.

💛 Proceeds benefit Bachman Community Center, a nonprofit that keeps a historic 1937 school building open as a free community resource, with a public library and space for local groups. The thrift store is volunteer-run and provides almost half of the center's operating budget.

Samaritan Center

Thrift store 9231 Lee Hwy, Ooltewah, TN 37363

Hours: Mon-Thu 9-6 · Fri/Sat Closed · Sun 1-5

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay

Reviews are mixed on the clearance section specifically, but many praise the low prices and friendly staff.

💛 Proceeds benefit the Samaritan Center, a community-service ministry over 90 years old, rooted in the Seventh-day Adventist church. It provides emergency assistance with rent, utilities, food, clothing, furniture, school supplies, and baby supplies for families across eastern Hamilton County, and serves as a regional collection point during disaster response efforts.

St. Timothy's Thrift Shop

Thrift store 627 Mississippi Ave, Signal Mountain, TN 37377

Hours: Mon Closed · Tue Closed · Wed 9:30-12 · Thu 3-5 · Fri/Sat 10-1 · Sun Closed

Price: $$ · Pay: Cash, checks, Venmo · Sales: End-of-season sales in winter and summer

Church-run thrift shop on Signal Mountain with clothing, household items and toys; sales fund local outreach.

💛 Proceeds benefit St. Timothy's Episcopal Church's outreach, including Family Promise, Welcome Home of Chattanooga, Metropolitan Ministries, Widows' Harvest, and scholarships for St. Timothy's Preschool.

The Clothes House on Signal Mountain

Thrift store 710 Signal Mountain Blvd, Signal Mountain, TN 37377

Hours: Sun-Tue Closed · Wed-Sat 9-1

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

Community thrift store on Signal Mountain with clothing and used goods, a quick, easy stop.

💛 Proceeds benefit Signal Mountain Social Services, which provides food assistance, educational support, and emergency help to local families and individuals.
