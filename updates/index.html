Trail Updates

The Chattanooga Thrift Trail keeps getting smarter. Here is how it has grown, one version at a time.

v33.0 The North Chatt Loop

  • New North Chatt Loop covers Signal Mountain, Hixson, Soddy-Daisy, Red Bank and North Shore. Find it in Filter.
  • Each shop card now shows the deal of the day, with every deal day a tap away.
  • Monthly deal days, like the 2nd Tuesday, now show up on the right day.

v32.0 More Info, Less Clutter

  • Every shop card has a More Info button that jumps to its full entry on Shops & Charities.
  • Deals are one tidy line. Tap it to see every sale in a pop-up.
  • Shop cards have cleaner titles, and Shops & Charities spells everything out with a Back to Top button on phones.

v31.0 Neighboring Towns

  • Dalton, Cleveland, Ringgold and 10 more towns are one tick away with Shops in Neighboring Towns.
  • Pick a single town and it becomes its own little trail.
  • Neighboring shops get their own hexagon so you always know who’s from out of town.

v30.0 Deals of the Day and Shop Summaries

  • Each shop card shows its sales, with today’s deal highlighted first.
  • A short summary of what shoppers say about every shop.
  • Star ratings now blend our visitors’ reviews with Google’s.

v29.0 Trail News and Shop Links

  • Trail News brings shop news, sales and press right to the home page.
  • Every shop has a Google Listing link for hours, photos and reviews.
  • Shops show their Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links.

v28.0 Pull to Refresh and a Steadier Map

  • Pull down on your phone to refresh the Trail.
  • A Hide Zoom button keeps the map on your whole route.
  • A fresh tagline every time you visit.

v27.0 Better Price Estimates

  • Pick an item’s condition and its estimated resale price updates.
  • Add details like brand or size for a closer estimate.

v26.0 Photo Prices and 50+ Shops

  • ThriftiVision reads your photo and shows an approximate eBay price.
  • A Profit Check helps you decide whether an item is worth buying.
  • Overstock Warehouse joins the Trail, bringing it to 50+ shops.

v25.0 A Smoother Finish

  • A finish message that fits every phone.
  • Plan a future day and see the right opening times.

v24.0 Smarter Closing-Soon Routes

  • A shop about to close moves earlier in your route.
  • Double-tap the map to see your whole route.
  • Share a link straight to a shop’s reviews.

v23.0 Easier Phone Cards

  • Shorter shop cards on phones.
  • Turn your phone sideways to see the route beside the map.

v22.0 Image Search and Take It With You

  • A new Image Search page: search by photo with Google, eBay or ThriftiVision.
  • Print the Trail, add it to your home screen, or send it to your phone.
  • A Featured Shop of the Day line above Your Route.

v21.0 Most Visited and a Reset Button

  • A chart of the most visited shops.
  • A Featured Shop of the Day card on the Shops & Charities page.
  • A Reset button for a fresh start.

v20.0 Always Current

  • A live countdown shows when a closed shop opens.
  • The Trail refreshes itself every time you open it.
  • The East Ridge Antique District lists every shop inside it.

v19.0 Featured Shops and Your Own Stops

  • A gold star marks the Featured Shop of the Day.
  • Add errands, lunch or appointments to your route.
  • The Arcade opened with Chattanooga games.

v18.0 More Icons and Friendlier Menus

  • More map icons, including a pickup truck.
  • A close button on the phone menu.
  • A Shop Managers form for local shops.

v17.0 Make the Trail Yours

  • Choose your own map icon.
  • Scroll the More Shops list and watch each shop light up on the map.
  • Shops about to close are easier to spot.

v16.0 Your Car on the Map

  • Your car follows you on the map.
  • Shops off your route show as diamonds you can tap to add.
  • Add Your Own Stop arrived.

v15.0 A Tidier Menu

  • The menu is grouped into Explore, Use the Trail and About.
  • A Back to Top button on long pages.

v14.0 One Button Runs the Trail

  • Tap to move on, double-tap to go back, hold for directions.
  • Print the Trail to take along.
  • A form for shops to send updates.

v13.0 Reviews and Daily Fun Facts

  • Tap the stars to read and add reviews.
  • A fun fact for every day.
  • Skip around and the route re-plans itself.

v12.0 Your Maps App, Your Way

  • Pick Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze once.
  • The big button takes you shop to shop.
  • Website links for every shop.

v11.0 Every Page Matches

  • Send Feedback from any page.
  • Add to Home Screen instructions for every phone and computer.
  • Sign up for the newsletter from the menu.

v10.0 Coffee, Neighborhoods and Loops

  • Add a coffee stop between shops.
  • A page for every neighborhood.
  • The Lookout Loop and Scenic City Loop.

v9.0 Filters, Calendar and Shop Details

  • Filter shops by kind and area.
  • A Community Calendar for yard sales and shop events.
  • Restrooms, wheelchair access and payments listed for each shop.

v8.0 Shops With a Purpose

  • A heart note shows which local cause a shop's proceeds support.
  • Hours, addresses and map pins were checked against each shop.
  • More shops joined the Trail.

v7.0 A Home of Its Own

  • The Trail moved to its own address, 423thrift.com, with a secure connection.
  • The Trail became easier to find in search.

v6.0 Take It Along

  • Add the Trail to your home screen and use it like an app.
  • Faster loading and basic offline support.

v5.0 Pick Up Where You Left Off

  • Mark shops visited and your progress is saved.
  • An unfinished trail resumes when you come back.

v4.0 Share the Trail

  • A Trail Poster and share buttons for Instagram, text and copy link.
  • A printable guide to take along.

v3.0 Reviews

  • Star ratings and short reviews for every shop.
  • Add a photo and mark reviews as helpful.

v2.0 Smarter Routes

  • The route follows opening hours and sets closed shops aside.
  • Plan for now or plan ahead for another day.
  • Miles and shops remaining.

v1.0 The Trail Begins

  • A loop route that starts from where you are.
