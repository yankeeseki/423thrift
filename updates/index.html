Trail Updates

The Chattanooga Thrift Trail keeps getting smarter. Here is how it has grown, one version at a time.

v22.0 Send It to Your Phone

  • Send To My Phone lets you build a route on a computer and open it on your phone, with a link, a QR code or an email.
  • A new blog post, How the Chattanooga Thrift Trail Helps Local Shops, Vendors and Charities, shows the numbers behind the Trail, with sources.

v21.0 Most Visited, Featured Shop and a Reset Button

  • A Most Visited Shops chart ranks the Trail's favorites, with flames on shops that are climbing fast and a gold bar for the Featured Shop of the Day.
  • A big Featured Shop of the Day card on the Shops & Charities page tells you about the shop in the spotlight.
  • Trail pages now show the date right at the start of your route line.
  • A simple Reset button takes you back to a fresh start at the top of the home page.

v20.0 Always Current, Always Open

  • Shops opening later now show a live "Opens in" countdown, and the route only includes a late opener when the wait is short.
  • More Shops lists what is open right now first, closest to you first.
  • The Trail refreshes itself when you open it, bring your phone out of sleep or come back from another app.
  • The big button now heads to your first shop before it moves on, so you can get directions to shop 1.
  • The East Ridge Antique District lists each shop inside it, and new shops joined the Trail.
  • A monthly Trail News post on the blog shares what is new.

v19.0 Featured Shops and Your Own Stops

  • The Featured Shop of the Day gets a gold star on the map and a line above the daily banner.
  • Add Your Own Stop lets you drop errands, lunch or appointments into your route, with address suggestions from about 2,500 local businesses.
  • A smarter route optimizer finds shorter loops by testing shop-by-shop swaps.
  • The Names on/off button returned to the map.
  • A copyright line and project protections were added across the site.
  • The Arcade opened with Chattanooga-themed games.

v18.0 Clearer and Friendlier

  • A close button on the phone menu makes it easier to get out of.
  • The one-button hint pulses to catch your eye.
  • Pick from a bigger list of map icons, including a pickup truck.
  • The Shop Managers form explains who the Trail is for.

v17.0 Make the Trail Yours

  • Choose your own map icon from the Account menu.
  • The car on the map gently pulses so you can find yourself.
  • Closing-soon shops are handled more carefully when your route is built.
  • Scrolling the More Shops list lights up each shop on the map as you go.

v16.0 Your Car on the Map

  • Your location refreshes itself while the Trail is open, and the car leaves a trail where you have been.
  • Every shop that is not on the loop shows on the map as a faded diamond you can tap and add.
  • The first version of Add Your Own Stop arrived.
  • Daily fun facts were refined.

v15.0 A Tidier Menu

  • The menu is grouped under Explore, Use the Trail and About.
  • A Back to Top button appears when you are far down the page.
  • Print the Trail and Get the Web App sit side by side.
  • Fixes to the arrow buttons on phones.

v14.0 One Button Runs the Trail

  • Tap to move on, double-tap to go back and press and hold for directions.
  • A Directions, Next and Undo row backs up the gestures.
  • Bigger touch areas make every button easier to tap.
  • Step-by-step location help for iPhone, Android and computers.
  • A Shop Managers form lets shops send updates to the moderators.
  • Print the Trail builds a guide you can take along.

v13.0 Reviews and Daily Fun Facts

  • Tap the stars on any shop to read its reviews and add your own.
  • Every day shows its special day with fun facts underneath.
  • Shop details match everywhere you see them.
  • Zoom buttons came back on the map, and ticking a shop out of order re-plans the rest of the route.

v12.0 Your Maps App, Your Way

  • Pick Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze once and the Trail remembers.
  • The big button walks you through your trip one shop at a time.
  • A line under the button always names the shop you are at.
  • Website links for every shop.

v11.0 Every Page Matches

  • Every page shares the same look, header, menu and footer.
  • Send Feedback opens right where you are.
  • Add to Home Screen instructions for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows.
  • The big button reads Continue to your next shop and opens directions.
  • Newsletter sign-up from the menu.

v10.0 Coffee, Neighborhoods and Loops

  • Add a coffee stop between shops, without it counting as a thrift stop.
  • A page for every neighborhood.
  • The Lookout Loop and Scenic City Loop were named.
  • A menu with your own optional Account.
  • Filters you can combine and the Trail remembers.
  • The Community Calendar became a month view with special days built in.
  • A gentle reminder to leave kind reviews for small businesses.

v9.0 Filters, Calendar and Shop Details

  • A Filter panel lets you choose the kinds of shops and areas you want.
  • A Community Calendar for yard sales, church sales and shop events.
  • Per-shop details: restrooms, wheelchair access, payments, Wi-Fi and sale days.
  • Price feel from $ to $$$$$.
  • The Trail updates itself quietly in the background.
  • World's Longest Yard Sale reminders.
  • A Get Involved form for fans, shops and volunteers.

v8.0 Shops With a Purpose

  • A heart note shows which local cause a shop's proceeds support.
  • Hours, addresses and map pins were checked against each shop.
  • More shops joined the Trail.

v7.0 A Home of Its Own

  • The Trail moved to its own address, 423thrift.com, with a secure connection.
  • The Trail became easier to find in search.

v6.0 Take It Along

  • Add the Trail to your home screen and use it like an app.
  • Faster loading and basic offline support.

v5.0 Pick Up Where You Left Off

  • Mark shops visited and your progress is saved.
  • An unfinished trail resumes when you come back.

v4.0 Share the Trail

  • A Trail Poster and share buttons for Instagram, text and copy link.
  • A printable guide to take along.

v3.0 Reviews

  • Star ratings and short reviews for every shop.
  • Add a photo and mark reviews as helpful.

v2.0 Smarter Routes

  • The route follows opening hours and sets closed shops aside.
  • Plan for now or plan ahead for another day.
  • Miles and shops remaining.

v1.0 The Trail Begins

  • An interactive map of thrift, vintage and antique shops around Chattanooga.
  • A loop route that starts from where you are.
