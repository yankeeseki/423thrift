1. Quick start

  1. Open the Chattanooga Thrift Trail on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap Let’s Go Thrifting! and allow location when asked. The Trail starts at your nearest open shop. If you say no to location, it starts with the shop that opens soonest.
  3. Follow the numbered list (or the numbered circles on the map) from shop 1 onward. Once your route is planned, the big button says Head to your first shop. Long press it for directions there, and tap it once when you arrive. Allowing location never skips a shop. A small line under the big button names your stop (“Current stop”). Use Get Directions on any shop to open driving directions. The first time, the Trail asks which maps app you use (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze) and remembers your answer. You can change it any time in the menu under Maps app.
  4. When you finish a shop, tap the big button. After you arrive at your first shop it says Continue to the next shop. Tap it when you finish a shop and it ticks the shop you just left as Visited and moves you on. The route and the miles update live. A quick press moves on. Tapped too soon? Double-tap the button to go back a step (repeat to go back further). Need directions? Long press the button to open directions to the shop shown on it. The small Directions, Next and Undo links under the button do the same things with a plain tap. You can also tick Visited by hand on any shop card.

That is all you need. Everything below is for making the Trail fit you.

2. How the route is built

The Trail takes every shop that is on the route and open right now, then puts them in a driving order that forms a loop. It starts where you are and carries you around the neighborhoods, so you end near where you began. Shops that are closed today, or that close before you could reach them, are set aside automatically in More Shops under the list.

The numbers at the top of the route are estimates. Miles are measured straight between your stops, so real driving can run a little longer. The time remaining counts down to when your last open shop closes, with 20 minutes held back to shop there. The minutes per shop is what is left after an estimated 25 mph of driving, split across the shops you have left.

The route recalculates live. When a shop closes for the day, it drops out; when you add, remove or reorder shops, the order and the total miles update on the spot.

Hours come from each shop’s listing and can change, especially around holidays, so confirm before you drive.

3. Choose your experience (Filter)

The small Filter link right under “Your Route” opens the filter panel, just below the orange Let’s Go Thrifting box. By default the Lookout Loop is checked. Uncheck it to choose your own mix; with nothing checked, the map is blank until you pick again or tap Reset. Change it to fit your mood:

  • Thrift shops: charity and donation-based thrift shops. This is the original Trail.
  • Curated shops: owner-curated vintage clothing, record shops, gift shops and vendor markets.
  • Antique malls: multi-booth antique and collectibles malls.
  • Lookout Loop: the moderators’ hand-picked favorites, across thrift, vintage and antique.
  • Scenic City Loop: the Tennessee shops from those favorites.

Not sure which kind of shop fits your day? Read Thrift, Vintage or Antique? Why the Trail Has Three Kinds of Shops, and for why vintage and antique prices run higher, What It Costs to Be a Vendor.

Mix and match. Every box you check adds its shops to your route, and no shop is ever listed twice. Tap Everything to check the three shop types at once. If you uncheck every box, the map and lists go empty on purpose; check one again or tap Reset.

Where the other shops go

Whatever you check becomes your route. Every other shop drops into the second list under the route. With the classic view it is called More Shops; with any other mix it is called All Other Shops. Pick Antique malls only, for example, and the thrift stores and vintage shops wait in that list, one tap on a plus button away from joining your route. Shops that are open right now are listed first, then everything else, and each group is sorted with the closest shop at the top. The map re-frames to fit exactly what is on your route.

Lookout Loop and Scenic City Loop

Check Lookout Loop or Scenic City Loop along with the other boxes to add the moderators’ hand-picked favorites to your route. Check it alone and you see only those shops, and you can still narrow them by area, open today and the other options.

Narrow it down

  • Exclude Experience: check Thrift shops, Curated shops or Antique malls here to hide that kind of shop from your route and map, even when it is part of Lookout Loop or Scenic City Loop.
  • Add Area: tap one or more neighborhoods, such as Hixson, Highland Park, East Ridge, Hwy 58 or Brainerd & Hamilton Place, to keep the route in that part of town.
  • Exclude Area: tap a neighborhood here to hide its shops from your route and map.
  • Open today: hides shops that are closed for the rest of the day.
  • Open late: only shops that close at 6 PM or later.
  • Supports a charity 💛: only shops whose proceeds benefit a cause. See Shops & Charities for who benefits.

The panel shows how many shops match. Your choices are saved on your device. Tap Reset to go back to the default, and the link shows a count, like Filter (2), whenever filters are on.

4. Reading the map and list

  • Numbered circles are your shops in order. The color shows the type: olive for thrift stores, rust for curated and vintage shops, gold for antique malls.
  • Gold star with a number is today’s Featured Shop.
  • Gold diamonds are bonus shops, which are off the main loop until you add them.
  • Names: the map shows numbers only so it stays clean. Tap a shop (or hover on a computer), on the map or in the list, and that number grows and its short name appears. Use the Names button at the bottom right to show every name at once. The map opens already framed to fit every shop on your route, and it re-frames when you add or remove one.
  • Open badges show whether a shop is open now and when it closes.
  • Shops that open later in the day join your route when you can reach them about when they open, with no more than about 12 minutes of waiting. A shop that opens too late for your loop waits in More Shops, where you can add it, and it rejoins your route as the day goes on.
  • Tap any marker or list card for the address, hours, notes and reviews.

5. Tailor the route to your interests

Every shop in the list has small controls. Use them to shape the day:

  • On a phone: the map sits at the top. A Back to map button floats on screen as you scroll the list, and the map gently drifts toward the shop you are scrolling past.
  • Remove a shop: tap the minus button. It moves to More Shops as “Removed by you” and the route recalculates.
  • Add a shop: open More Shops and tap the plus button on any gold-diamond shop to bring it into today’s plan. Tap the plus on a removed shop to put it back.
  • Start somewhere else: tap the ▶ button on any shop to make it your starting point. The loop is rebuilt from there.
  • Reorder by hand: use the up and down arrows to move a shop earlier or later. A “Custom order” bar appears, and Reset returns to the automatic order.
  • Search for a shop: type in the search box at the top of the shop list. It shows every shop with a word in its name that starts with what you typed (type “g” to see every Goodwill), and it narrows as you type. Tap the × to bring the full list back. It is handy for jumping straight to the shop where you want to start.
  • Start Over: the “Your changes are saved on this device” bar has a Start over button that clears today’s visited shops and your adds and removals.
  • Reset: at the bottom of the page is a button that wipes everything the Trail saved on your device, reloads like a first visit and asks for your location again. Use it if the Trail ever feels stuck.

Five ready-made recipes

The bargain hunter

Filter: Thrift stores only. Remove the shops you have visited lately and pick one neighborhood, such as Brainerd or Hixson, for a short loop.

The vintage lover

Filter: Curated shops plus Antique malls. Start at Poor Taste downtown or in the North Shore cluster and let the Trail link them up.

The shop-with-purpose day

Filter: Supports a charity. Tap Done and read the 💛 notes in Shops & Charities to see where your money goes.

The after-work run

Filter: Open late. Plan for the evening with the Later tab (see below) and check closing times before you leave.

The full day

Filter: Everything. Remove anything that does not interest you, add a bonus shop or two and set a lunch break in the middle.

6. Plan ahead for another day

Above the list, switch from Today to Later to plan a future day. Pick a date and a start time, and the Trail builds the plan using that day’s hours, so shops that are closed that day are set aside. Most shops close by 6 to 8 PM, so an early start gives you the longest day.

You can share a plan with a friend. The shared link carries your added and removed shops so they see the same route.

7. Track your progress

Tap the big Continue to button, or tick the Visited box on a shop card, as you finish each shop. Visited shops are crossed out and the remaining miles update. Your visited shops and any changes you made are saved on your device and come back if you refresh the page.

If you come back on another day, the Trail offers to pick up where you left off and skips the shops you already visited. If your browser is in private mode, it may not be able to save, and the Trail will tell you.

8. Take it with you and share it

  • Print the Trail: a one-page list of today’s shops in route order, with addresses and hours. Good for when your phone battery is low.
  • Share the Trail: send it by Instagram, Facebook, X, text or copy the link, or open the Trail Poster.
  • Trail Poster: opens the poster image so you can save or print it.
  • Instagram: opens your phone’s share sheet with the Trail poster ready to post.

9. Reviews and the Featured Shop

Open a shop card to read what other thrifters say, and leave your own rating or review. Each day one shop gets the gold star as the Featured Shop. Poor Taste is the Saturday feature.

10. Add it to your home screen

The Trail is a web app: nothing to download and no account. To get it on your phone, open it in Safari on iPhone, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. It then opens full screen like an app. See the blog for step-by-step help.

11. Troubleshooting

It says no shops match

Your filters may be too narrow, or the shops that match are closed. Tap Reset in the Filter panel.

Location is not working

If you tapped Don’t Allow, the site cannot ask again. Turn location back on in your phone or browser, then reload the page and tap the big button again:

  • iPhone (Safari): tap the aA icon in the address bar, then Website Settings, then Location, and choose Allow. If it is still off, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Safari Websites and choose While Using the App.
  • iPhone (added to your home screen): go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services, find the Thrift Trail or Safari Websites entry, and choose While Using the App.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the icon at the left of the address bar, then Permissions (or Site settings), then Location, and choose Allow.
  • Computer (Chrome): click the icon at the left of the address bar, open Site settings, set Location to Allow, then reload.
  • Computer (Safari): go to Safari > Settings > Websites > Location, find 423thrift.com and choose Allow.

Without location, the Trail starts with the shop that opens soonest, and you can tap ▶ on any shop to choose your own start.

I tapped the big button too soon

Double-tap the button, or tap Undo under it, and the shop goes back to not visited. Each double-tap or Undo goes back one more step. You can also untick it in the Visited list under your route.

Can I build my route on a computer and use it on my phone?

Yes. On a computer, build your route, then under Take It With You tap Send To My Phone. Scan the code with your phone’s camera, or copy the link or email it to yourself. Your phone opens with your shops, their order, your own stops and your start point. It is a one-time copy, so later changes on either device do not sync. Anyone with the link can see the names and addresses of your own stops. The link opens in your phone’s browser, not in a Trail you added to your home screen (that copy keeps its own saved route), so keep going in the browser for that trip.

Can I add my own stop, like lunch or an errand?

Yes. Below the shop list, in the Your own stops box, tap ➕ Add Your Own Stop. Start typing a name and pick a common Chattanooga place from the suggestions, or type a street address, then choose an icon. The Trail places the stop for you. If you add an appointment time, it goes where you can finish the shops before it, and the card shows about when to leave (an estimate, with no live traffic). Without a time, it goes where it adds the least driving. Your stop stays on your device only and clears itself the next day.

Can I add a coffee stop?

Yes. Tap ☕ Add a coffee stop, right beside Add Your Own Stop below the shop list, and the Trail offers up to three open coffee shops on the way to your next shop, with the moderators’ favorites first. Tap Add to Route to drop one in before that shop, or Directions to go straight there. Coffee shops also show on the map with a ☕ marker.

Do I have to refresh to get the newest version?

No. The Trail checks for updates by itself when you open it, when you come back to it, and every few minutes. When a new version is ready the page quietly refreshes itself at a safe moment, with no banner and nothing to tap. Your progress is saved first.

The Trail feels stuck or out of date

Scroll to the bottom of the page, tap Reset, then confirm. It clears everything saved on your device and starts fresh. If you added the Trail to your home screen, this works there too.

My progress did not save

Private or incognito browsing can block saving. Open the Trail in a regular tab.

A shop is closed or the hours are wrong

Hours come from listings and can change. Call the shop or check its page, and remove it from your route with the minus button.

12. Quick answers

Why use the Trail instead of Google Maps?

Google Maps limits how many stops you can add to a route, and leaves the order up to you. The Trail has no built-in shop limit and orders your loop around each shop’s hours. Each shop’s Get directions link opens the maps app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), so the two work together. Read why not just use Google Maps.

Does the Thrift Trail cost anything?

Not right now. The tip buttons at the bottom are optional.

Do I need to download an app or make an account?

No. It is a web app that runs in your browser. Add it to your home screen if you want it to open like an app (see Get the web app above).

How many shops are on it?

more than forty thrift stores, vintage shops and antique malls around Chattanooga and nearby North Georgia.

Can I see every shop at once?

Yes. Open Shops & Charities for the full list by neighborhood with addresses, hours and the cause each charity shop supports.

Where can I find yard sales and local events?

Open the Community Calendar. It shows church sales, shop events and special thrift days by month, with event names right in the calendar. Anyone can submit an in-person event with a street address.

I run a shop. How do I update my listing?

Open Update Your Shop Listing (also in the menu under Shop managers). Tell the moderators your hours, sales and what shoppers should know, and they review it before changing anything. You can also download the Trail Poster there to display in your shop.

Should I bring cash or a card?

Bring a little of each. Many shops take cards, and some set a card minimum or add a small card fee. The “Pay” line on a shop’s card shows what it accepts. Here is why a little cash helps everyone.

Which maps app does the Trail use?

Your choice. The first time you tap Get directions, the Trail asks whether you use Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze and remembers it. Change it any time in the menu under Maps app.
