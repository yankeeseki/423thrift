How the Thrift Trail Works

1. Quick start

  1. Open the Chattanooga Thrift Trail on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap Let’s Go Thrifting! and allow location when asked. The Trail starts at your nearest open shop. If you say no to location, it starts with the shop that opens soonest.
  3. Follow the numbered list (or the numbered circles on the map) from stop 1 onward. Use Get directions on any shop to open driving directions.
  4. Tick Visited as you go. The route and the miles update live.

That is all you need. Everything below is for making the Trail fit you.

2. How the route is built

The Trail takes every shop that is on the route and open right now, then puts them in a driving order that forms a loop. It starts where you are and carries you around the neighborhoods, so you end near where you began. Shops that are closed today, or that close before you could reach them, are set aside automatically in Bonus & Closed Shops under the list.

The route recalculates live. When a shop closes for the day, it drops out; when you add, remove or reorder shops, the order and the total miles update on the spot.

Hours come from each shop’s listing and can change, especially around holidays, so confirm before you drive.

3. Choose your experience (Filter)

The small Filter link right under “Your Route” opens the filter panel, just below the orange Let’s Go Thrifting box. By default the Trail shows the classic thrift-store loop. Change it to fit your mood:

  • Thrift stores: charity and donation-based thrift stores. This is the original Trail.
  • Curated & vintage: owner-curated vintage clothing, record shops, gift shops and vendor markets.
  • Antique malls: multi-booth antique and collectibles malls.
  • Everything: all of the above in one route.

Mix and match. Check Thrift stores and Curated together, for example. At least one type always stays checked.

Mod’s Top 25

Check ⭐ Mod’s Top 25 at the top of the panel to see only the shops hand-picked by the Trail’s owner, across thrift, vintage and antique. It overrides the shop-type boxes, and you can still narrow it by area, open today and the other options.

Narrow it down

  • Area: tap one or more neighborhoods, such as Hixson, Red Bank or Southside, to keep the route in that part of town.
  • Open today: hides shops that are closed for the rest of the day.
  • Open late: only shops that close at 6 PM or later.
  • Supports a charity 💛: only shops whose proceeds benefit a cause. See Shops & Charities for who benefits.
  • Shops I haven’t visited: hides shops you have already checked off.

The panel shows how many shops match. Your choices are saved on your device. Tap Reset to go back to the default, and the link shows a count, like Filter (2), whenever filters are on.

4. Reading the map and list

  • Numbered circles are your stops in order. The color shows the type: olive for thrift stores, rust for curated and vintage shops, gold for antique malls.
  • Gold star with a number is today’s Featured Shop.
  • Gold diamonds are bonus shops, which are off the main loop until you add them.
  • Names: the map shows numbers only so it stays clean. Tap a stop (or hover on a computer), on the map or in the list, and that number grows and its short name appears. Use the Names button at the bottom right to show every name at once, and Whole route at the top right to zoom back out.
  • Open badges show whether a shop is open now and when it closes.
  • Tap any marker or list card for the address, hours, notes and reviews.

5. Tailor the route to your interests

Every shop in the list has small controls. Use them to shape the day:

  • On a phone: the map sits at the top. A Back to map button floats on screen as you scroll the list, and the map gently drifts toward the shop you are scrolling past.
  • Remove a shop: tap the minus button. It moves to Bonus & Closed Shops as “Removed by you” and the route recalculates.
  • Add a shop: open Bonus & Closed Shops and tap the plus button on any gold-diamond shop to bring it into today’s plan. Tap the plus on a removed shop to put it back.
  • Start somewhere else: tap the ▶ button on any stop to make it your starting point. The loop is rebuilt from there.
  • Reorder by hand: use the up and down arrows to move a shop earlier or later. A “Custom order” bar appears, and Reset returns to the automatic order.
  • Start over: the “Your changes are saved on this device” bar has a Start over button that clears today’s visited shops and your adds and removals.

Five ready-made recipes

The bargain hunter

Filter: Thrift stores only. Remove the shops you have visited lately and pick one neighborhood, such as Brainerd or Hixson, for a short loop.

The vintage lover

Filter: Curated & vintage plus Antique malls. Start at Poor Taste downtown or in the North Shore cluster and let the Trail link them up.

The shop-with-purpose day

Filter: Supports a charity. Tap Done and read the 💛 notes in Shops & Charities to see where your money goes.

The after-work run

Filter: Open late. Plan for the evening with the Later tab (see below) and check closing times before you leave.

The full day

Filter: Everything. Remove anything that does not interest you, add a bonus shop or two and set a lunch break in the middle.

6. Plan ahead for another day

Above the list, switch from Today to Later to plan a future day. Pick a date and a start time, and the Trail builds the plan using that day’s hours, so shops that are closed that day are set aside. Most stores close by 6 to 8 PM, so an early start gives you the longest day.

You can share a plan with a friend. The shared link carries your added and removed shops so they see the same route.

7. Track your progress

Tick the Visited box on each stop as you finish. Visited shops are crossed out and the remaining miles update. Your visited shops and any changes you made are saved on your device and come back if you refresh the page.

If you come back on another day, the Trail offers to pick up where you left off and skips the shops you already visited. If your browser is in private mode, it may not be able to save, and the Trail will tell you.

8. Take it with you and share it

  • Printable Trail Guide (PDF): a one-page list of today’s shops in route order, with addresses and hours. Good for when your phone battery is low.
  • Share the Trail: send it by Facebook, X, text or copy the link.
  • Trail Poster: opens the poster image so you can save or print it.
  • Instagram: opens your phone’s share sheet with the Trail poster ready to post.

9. Reviews and the Featured Shop

Open a shop card to read what other thrifters say, and leave your own rating or review. Each day one shop gets the gold star as the Featured Shop. Poor Taste is the Saturday feature.

10. Add it to your home screen

On iPhone, open the Trail in Safari, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. It then opens full screen like an app. See the blog for step-by-step help.

11. Troubleshooting

It says no shops match

Your filters may be too narrow, or the shops that match are closed. Tap Reset in the Filter panel.

Location is not working

Allow location for the site in your browser settings. Without it, the Trail starts with the shop that opens soonest, and you can tap ▶ on any stop to choose your own start.

My progress did not save

Private or incognito browsing can block saving. Open the Trail in a regular tab.

A shop is closed or the hours are wrong

Hours come from listings and can change. Call the shop or check its page, and remove it from your route with the minus button.

12. Quick answers

Is the Thrift Trail free?

Yes. The tip buttons at the bottom are optional.

Do I need to download an app or make an account?

No. It runs in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen.

How many shops are on it?

More than thirty thrift stores, vintage shops and antique malls around Chattanooga and nearby North Georgia.

Can I see every shop at once?

Yes. Open Shops & Charities for the full list by neighborhood with addresses, hours and the cause each charity shop supports.
