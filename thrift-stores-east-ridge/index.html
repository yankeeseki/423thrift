Thrift Stores & Vintage Shops in East Ridge, TN

Here are the 7 thrift, vintage and secondhand shops on the Chattanooga Thrift Trail in East Ridge and just over the line in Rossville, GA: 2 thrift stores, 3 vintage shops and 2 antique shops. Each has its address, hours and what reviewers say.

Our vintage and antique shops are well curated, each vendor with a style of their own. Just don’t expect thrift prices.

Chattanooga Room in the Inn Thrift Store

Thrift store 3807 Ringgold Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun 1-6

Price: $$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay · No card minimum · Free Wi-Fi · Sales: Sun 50-90% off · Mon 50% off select items · Tue-Thu 75% off select items · Wed also 10% off for seniors and veterans · Sat 90% off items

Opened in September 2025 next to the East Ridge Goodwill; every dollar supports shelter and stability for women and children experiencing homelessness. Earn Clover loyalty points.

💛 Proceeds benefit Chattanooga Room in the Inn, which runs a six-month live-in program for women and children experiencing homelessness, with housing, case management, and education and employment support. The thrift store supports the programs at the shelter.

East Ridge Antique District

Antique 6511 Slater Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon 10:30-5:30 · Tue-Fri 10:30-5 · Sat 10:30-4 · Sun 12-5

Price: $$$$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards (3% card fee)

Most of the antique district has closed. Three shops are still open: Sugar Plum Antiques & Treasures (Mon-Sat 10-6, Sun 12-6), Rusted Rooster Antiques & Decor (Mon closed, Tue-Fri 10-5, Sat 10-4, Sun 11-5) and Chattanooga Olive Oil Company & Artisan Village (Mon-Sat 10:30-5:30, Sun 12-5:30). The hours listed are when every open shop is available, so check each shop's hours, and some close as early as 4.

Goodwill (East Ridge)

Thrift store 3715 Ringgold Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon-Sat 9-8 · Sun 10-8

Price: $$$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards · No card minimum

A newer, huge location with well-organized racks and friendly staff on a good day, though reviewers note prices running higher than expected and inconsistent organization at busier times.

💛 Proceeds benefit Goodwill Industries' job training, vocational skills, and employment placement programs, with a particular focus on people who face barriers to finding work.
