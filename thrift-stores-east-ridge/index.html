Chattanooga Room in the Inn Thrift Store

Thrift store 3807 Ringgold Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun 1-6

Price: $$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

Opened in September 2025 next to the East Ridge Goodwill; every dollar supports shelter and stability for women and children experiencing homelessness.

💛 Proceeds benefit Chattanooga Room in the Inn, which provides shelter, support, and stability to women and children experiencing homelessness in Chattanooga.

East Ridge Antique District

Antique mall 6511 Slater Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun 11-6

Price: $$$$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay

Large multi-booth antique district with a wide variety and reviewers calling prices reasonable for what's found. Each shop sets its own hours, and visitors have suggested some may close at 5pm, so go earlier in the day.

Goodwill (East Ridge)

Thrift store 3715 Ringgold Rd, East Ridge, TN 37412

Hours: Mon-Sat 9-8 · Sun 10-8

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

A newer, huge location with well-organized racks and friendly staff on a good day, though reviewers note prices running higher than expected and inconsistent organization at busier times.

💛 Proceeds benefit Goodwill Industries' job training, vocational skills, and employment placement programs, with a particular focus on people who face barriers to finding work.
