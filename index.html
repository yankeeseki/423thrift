Your Customizable and Automatically Optimized Thrift Store Treasure Map!

The route: A loop through Southside, Rossville, Fort Oglethorpe, Brainerd, East Brainerd, Hamilton Place, Hixson, Red Bank & North Shore — join wherever you are, and it carries you back to your start.

How it works: Tap Let's Go Thrifting! to build today's plan from wherever you are. Closed stores are set aside automatically, and you can add or remove any shop yourself.

Bonus shops: Gold diamonds are extra shops worth a visit, off the main loop. Tap + Add to Route to bring any of them into today's plan.

More stops than Google Maps: Google Maps limits how many stops a route can have. The Trail has no built-in limit and orders your loop around each shop’s hours. Why not just use Google Maps?

Add the Trail to Your Home Screen

Open the Thrift Trail with one tap, like an app, with no web address to type. And you never need to download an update. It’ll be automatic!

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari, not inside Facebook, Instagram, or another app’s built-in browser.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see it, tap View More first.
  4. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if you see that instead).
  4. Tap Add or Install.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen.

Mac

  1. Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): open the File menu and choose Add to Dock…, then click Add.
  2. Chrome: click the three dots, then Save and share (older versions: Cast, save, and share), then Install page as app.

On an iPhone, the home-screen shortcut can keep its own saved progress, separate from what you did in Safari.

Chattanooga thrift stores, vintage shops & antique malls

Chattanooga Thrift Trail is a self-guided map of 44 secondhand shops around Chattanooga, Tennessee and nearby North Georgia, from Southside, Downtown and North Shore to Brainerd, Hixson, Red Bank, East Ridge, Ooltewah and Fort Oglethorpe. Tap the start button and the Trail builds the best driving order from where you are, around each shop’s hours.
