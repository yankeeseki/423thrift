Get the Trail Web App

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with one tap. Nothing to download, no account, and updates arrive on their own.

This is the icon you’ll see on your home screen, labeled Thrift Trail.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari, not inside Facebook, Instagram, or another app’s built-in browser.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see it, tap View More first.
  4. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if you see that instead).
  4. Tap Add or Install.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen.

Mac

  1. Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): open the File menu and choose Add to Dock…, then click Add.
  2. Chrome: click the three dots, then Save and share (older versions: Cast, save, and share), then Install page as app.

Windows PC

  1. Chrome: click the three dots, then Save and share (older versions: Cast, save, and share), then Install page as app.
  2. Edge: click the three dots, then Apps, then Install this site as an app.

On an iPhone, the home-screen shortcut can keep its own saved progress, separate from what you did in Safari.

The Chattanooga Thrift Trail exists to make it easy to find good deals. It is a self-guided map of thrift shops, vintage shops and antique shops around the Chattanooga area. It builds an efficient driving route around each shop’s hours, so you can visit the most shops with the least driving, and it offers hand-picked loops, the Lookout Loop and the Scenic City Loop, for a nice drive.

Chattanooga thrift stores, vintage shops & antique shops

Chattanooga Thrift Trail is a self-guided map of 48 secondhand shops around Chattanooga, Tennessee and nearby North Georgia, from Southside, Downtown and North Shore to Brainerd, Hixson, Red Bank, East Ridge, Ooltewah and Fort O. Tap the start button and the Trail builds the best driving order from where you are, around each shop’s hours.
