Edith's Emporium

Vintage / curated 1914 McCallie Ave, Chattanooga, TN 37404

Hours: Mon-Fri 11-5 · Sat 10-4 · Sun 12-4

Price: $$$$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay

Near-perfect reviews for its eclectic mix of gifts, home decor, and locally made goods.

Habitat For Humanity ReStore

Thrift store 1150 E 14th St, Chattanooga, TN 37408

Hours: Mon Closed · Tue-Sat 9-5 · Sun Closed

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash

Furniture and building materials with proceeds funding local home builds; reviews are mixed on pricing for the condition of some pieces.

💛 Proceeds benefit Habitat for Humanity's construction of affordable housing, built in partnership with the families who will live in them.

Get directions · Website

THE REFINDERY on McCallie

Vintage / curated 1300 McCallie Ave, Chattanooga, TN 37404

Hours: Daily 11-6

Price: $$$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash
