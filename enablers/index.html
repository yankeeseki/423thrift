Thrift Addiction Enablers

Let’s be honest: nobody needs to drive across town to dig through a bin of mystery crockery. We do it because we can’t stop. The Trail exists to feed the habit, and these local businesses decided to feed it right along with us.

A good friend would stage an intervention. An Enabler hands you a coffee at 9 a.m. so you’re awake when the Goodwill opens, then a sandwich at 1 so you can make it to the antique mall before it closes. They have never once asked how many lamps you own. That is why we call them Enablers, and why we are so grateful.

They keep the Trail running, the map current and the thrifters fueled. Stop in, say the Trail sent you, and tip well. Your habit owes them one.

Our first Enablers are on their way. Check back soon.
