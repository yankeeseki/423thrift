America's Thrift Stores (Brainerd)

Thrift store 5844 Brainerd Rd, Chattanooga, TN 37411

Hours: Mon-Thu 8-9 · Fri-Sat 8-10 · Sun 10-8

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

Clean and well organized, but a common theme in reviews is that prices run higher than expected for a thrift store.

💛 Proceeds benefit primarily Adult & Teen Challenge MidSouth, a faith-based addiction recovery program, alongside other local nonprofit partners focused on children's health and job readiness. The chain also reports keeping over 60 million pounds of donated goods out of landfills each year.

Chambliss Thrift Store (Brainerd)

Thrift store 3723 Brainerd Rd, Chattanooga, TN 37411

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun 1-6

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay

Rotating inventory with a good plus-size and kids selection, though reviews are mixed on furniture and electronics.

💛 Proceeds benefit Chambliss Center for Children's foster care and childcare programs, including a Transitional Living Program for youth aging out of foster care and a partnership establishing Hamilton County's first Isaiah 117 House, a safe space for children awaiting placement after being removed from unsafe homes.

Providence Thrift Store (Lee Hwy)

Thrift store 6933 Lee Hwy, Chattanooga, TN 37421

Hours: Mon-Sat 9-8 · Sun Closed

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards

Reviews are sharply divided — some find great deals and friendly staff, others describe the store as cluttered and poorly kept.

💛 Proceeds benefit Providence Ministries, which fights homelessness, hunger, addiction, and spiritual poverty. Money helps fund homeless shelters for men and women, a women's addiction recovery program, and a "To Feed the Hungry" meal program.

Savers

Thrift store 2020 Gunbarrel Rd Ste 198, Chattanooga, TN 37421

Hours: Mon-Sat 9-9 · Sun 10-7

Price: $$$ · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay

National for-profit thrift chain with a large, well-organized selection. Shopping does not fund a charity, but donations dropped off there benefit the Purple Heart Foundation. It's self-checkout, no bags are provided and carts can't leave the store, so bring your own bags if you're buying a lot.
