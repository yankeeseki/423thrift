Thrift Stores & Vintage Shops in Red Bank, TN

Here are the 5 thrift, vintage and secondhand shops on the Chattanooga Thrift Trail in Red Bank: 3 thrift stores and 2 antique shops. Each has its address, hours and what reviewers say.

Our vintage and antique shops are well curated, each vendor with a style of their own. Just don’t expect thrift prices.

Chambliss Thrift (Red Bank)

Thrift store 1936 Dayton Blvd, Red Bank, TN 37415

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun 1-6

Price: $$$ · Restrooms: No · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay · Rewards program

Well organized with furniture in unusually good condition, plus handmade items from local makers.

💛 Proceeds benefit Chambliss Center for Children's foster care and childcare programs, including a Transitional Living Program for youth aging out of foster care and a partnership establishing Hamilton County's first Isaiah 117 House, a safe space for children awaiting placement after being removed from unsafe homes.

NNH Thrift (Red Bank)

Thrift store 3605 Dayton Blvd, Red Bank, TN 37415

Hours: Mon-Sat 10-6 · Sun Closed

Price: $$$ · Restrooms: Yes · ♿ Wheelchair: Yes · Pay: Cash, credit & debit cards, tap to pay · Free Wi-Fi

Known for solid furniture donations at reasonable prices, supporting the nonprofit's community programs.

💛 Proceeds benefit Northside Neighborhood House's neighbor-assistance programs across the community. Thrift store sales make up almost half of NNH's total income.
