🏪 Small Business Saturday: Shop Local at Chattanooga Thrift and Vintage Shops

Holiday November 15, 2027 · 2 min read

Small Business Saturday, the Saturday after Thanksgiving, is a day to shop independent, locally owned businesses. For gift shoppers in Chattanooga, it is also a great chance to skip the crowds and find something unique. Many of the shops on the Thrift Trail are independent, owner-run and happy to see you.

Why shop small

  • Your money stays in the community.
  • You find items no big retailer carries.
  • Staff can tell you the story behind a piece.
  • You support the people who work and live here.

Where to go

Owner-run shops on the Trail include Poor Taste downtown, Edith's Emporium on McCallie Ave, Collective Clothing and Local Dive Retail on the North Shore, Vinterest in Hixson, and Lookout Thrift on the Southside, which reviewers call a beloved owner-run shop with real designer finds at thrift prices. Antique malls such as Dirty Jane's Antiques and The Refindery are full of independent vendors.

Make it a tradition

Pick three shops, set a budget and give gifts that came from them. Add a handwritten note about where each one was found.

Skip the crowds

While big-box parking lots fill up, small shops are calm and the owners have time to talk. It is a nice way to start the holiday season.

Quick answers

When is Small Business Saturday?

The Saturday after Thanksgiving.

Which shops are independent?

Many of the vintage and antique shops on the Trail are locally owned. Charity thrift stores support nonprofits.

Thrift tip: Check each shop's website or social media for holiday sales and special hours. Many run promotions this weekend.

Plan your Saturday on the Chattanooga thrift trail map and see every shop that is open.
