🏷️ Yard Sales and Thrift Shops: Do Both in One Weekend

Tips May 21, 2029 · 3 min read

Late spring is yard sale season, and it overlaps neatly with thrift shopping. The two complement each other: yard sales for bargains and surprises, thrift shops for reliable hours and a steady selection.

Start early, finish with the shops

Yard sales open early and the best finds go first, so hit them in the morning. By midday many are winding down, which is when thrift shops are open and ready. Use the Trail to plan the afternoon.

Bring cash, and small bills

Most yard sales run on cash. Bring small bills and some coins so that you can pay exact prices and negotiate politely. Many thrift shops take cards, but some add a fee or a minimum, so check the details on the Trail.

Plan your yard sale route

Pick an area with several sales close together, then add your own stops for them. Keep the list short, since addresses change and sales end early.

Carry what you find

  • A box or bin in the trunk for fragile items.
  • Blankets for furniture.
  • A tape measure and a list of sizes.

Be a good neighbor

Do not block driveways, and park where you will not be in the way. Be kind about prices. Remember that a yard sale is somebody’s home.

Thrift tip: Save a few dollars for the last shop of the day. Something always catches your eye.

Open the Trail, build the afternoon and enjoy a weekend of treasure hunting.
