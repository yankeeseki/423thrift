🛍️ National Thrift Store Day (August 17): How to Celebrate on the Thrift Trail

Holiday August 16, 2027 · 2 min read

August 17 is National Thrift Store Day, and it is the Trail's favorite holiday. It is a day to celebrate secondhand shopping, the people who donate and the nonprofits that put thrift money to work. Here is how to make the most of it.

Five ways to celebrate

  • Do a full Trail day. Pick a loop and visit at least four shops.
  • Donate. Clean out a closet and drop it off at a charity shop.
  • Buy one thing you love. Skip the practical and choose something that sparks joy.
  • Learn the story. Check the "Proceeds benefit" notes in the Shops & Charities list and see where your money goes.
  • Share it. Post your haul and tag the shop.

Where to start

If you are new to thrifting, start with a big charity thrift store. If you want a curated experience, try Poor Taste downtown or Collective Clothing on the North Shore. Combine one of each and you have a day.

Check for special deals

Some shops run sales or specials for the holiday. Check each shop's website or social media before you go.

Why it matters

Thrift stores keep goods in use, fund local nonprofits and give shoppers affordable options. The day, also known as National Thrift Shop Day, is a good excuse to visit a favorite and try a new one.

Quick answers

What date is National Thrift Store Day?

August 17.

How can I celebrate on a budget?

Visit a shop, buy one thing you love and donate something you no longer use.

Thrift tip: Take a friend and set a $20 challenge. Each person buys one item for the other.
