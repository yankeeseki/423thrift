⛰️ The Lookout Loop: A Moderator-Curated Thrift Route Through Chattanooga

Guide November 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Planning a thrift day usually means a pile of tabs, a list of hours and a lot of guessing about which shop to hit first. The Lookout Loop does that work for you. It is the Chattanooga Thrift Trail’s main route: a hand-picked set of shops, put in a sensible driving order, starting from wherever you are.

What the Lookout Loop is

The moderators chose the shops on the Lookout Loop themselves. There are 26 of them, mostly thrift shops, plus a few vintage favorites, and they stretch from Fort Oglethorpe and East Ridge through Brainerd and Highway 58 to Red Bank, Hixson, the North Shore and Southside. It is the biggest of the Trail’s routes. The Scenic City Loop is the smaller one, with only the Tennessee shops from those favorites.

How it starts

  1. Open the Thrift Trail. The Lookout Loop is already checked.
  2. Allow your location when your phone asks. You will see where you are on the map, and a moderator-curated route is suggested to you, beginning with a shop that is open near you.
  3. Follow the numbers. Each shop is a numbered stop on the map and in the list, in driving order, and the open badge on each card tells you whether it is open now and when it closes.

Prefer not to share your location? You can still use the Trail. Pick your starting shop with Start Here on any card.

Make it yours

The suggested route is a starting point, and it is meant to be changed. Customize it to your preferences:

  • Remove a shop with the minus button, or add one back from More Shops.
  • Move a stop up or down with the arrows when you would rather visit it sooner or later.
  • Narrow it down with Filter. Keep only the shops in one part of town, only the ones that are open late, or only the shops that support a charity.
  • Add your own stop, such as lunch or an errand, or add a coffee stop on the way to your next shop.

On the road

Tick Visited as you finish each shop and the Trail re-plans the rest of your route from where you are. The big button at the top always points to your next shop. Tap it to continue, double-tap to undo, or hold it for directions.

Thrift tip: Twenty-six shops is a lot for one day. Most people pick a stretch of the loop, such as East Ridge and Brainerd on one trip and Red Bank and the North Shore on another, and come back for the rest.

The loop changes as the moderators find new favorites, so it is worth another look every few weeks.
