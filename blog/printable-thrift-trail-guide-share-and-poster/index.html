🖨️ Take It With You: The Printable Trail Guide, Sharing and the Trail Poster

How-To June 5, 2028 · 2 min read

The Thrift Trail does more than show you a map. You can take your route with you on paper, send it to a friend and even print a poster for your own shop. Here is a tour of the extras near the bottom of the page.

Take It With You: the printable guide

Tap Download Printable Trail Guide (PDF) to get a one-page list of today's shops, in route order, with addresses and hours. It reflects the route you built, including any shops you added or removed. Print it for the glove box or keep the PDF on your phone as a backup when you have no signal.

Share the Trail

Know someone who would love a day of treasure hunting? Use the share buttons to send them the Trail.

  • Facebook and X: post a link with a preview picture.
  • Text It: opens a text message with the link ready to send.
  • Copy Link: paste it anywhere.
  • Instagram: on a phone, opens the share sheet with the Trail poster so you can post it to a Story, a Post or a Reel, or send it in a message.

The Trail Poster

If you run or work at a shop on the Trail, you can print the poster for your window or counter. Tap Trail Poster to open the print version, then download or print it. The QR code on the poster takes people straight to the map.

Tips

  • Print the guide before you head out, especially if you will be in areas with weak signal.
  • Post your haul and tag the shop. Shops love it.
  • Use the poster in a window facing the street, at eye level.

Share it with friends

The share buttons use the Trail poster so friends see something beautiful, not just a link. Post it to Instagram, Facebook or anywhere else.

Quick answers

Is the poster free?

Yes. Open it, then save the image.

Can I print the guide?

Yes. The guide lists your stops without a map.

Thrift tip: Tag the shops you visit when you post. It helps small businesses get found.
