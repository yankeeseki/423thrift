🏺 Shop Spotlight: Edith's Emporium on McCallie Ave

Spotlight May 15, 2028 · 2 min read

Some Chattanooga thrift and vintage shops are best known for their racks. Edith's Emporium, at 1914 McCallie Ave, is known for its variety. Reviewers describe an eclectic mix of gifts, home decor and locally made goods, and the shop earns near-perfect ratings for it.

What you will find

Think of it as a curated shop where vintage, handmade and local all share the same shelves. It is a good place to find a hostess gift, a conversation-starting piece of decor or something small for yourself. If you like a store where you do not have to dig, this is a good one.

Why it pairs well with a McCallie Ave loop

McCallie Avenue is home to a few of the Trail's best stops. The Refindery on McCallie, at 1300 McCallie Ave, is a cavernous antique mall that reviewers call a fun treasure hunt. Put the two together and you have a morning that covers both curated and wander-for-an-hour shopping. See the full Highland Park shop list.

Tips for shopping Edith's Emporium

  • Go with a gift list in mind. Locally made items sell and rotate, so if you see something you love, grab it.
  • Look at the small things near the register. They are often the best gifts.
  • Ask about local makers. Shop staff usually know the story behind what they carry.

Locally made gifts

The shop is known for an eclectic mix of gifts, home decor and locally made goods, which makes it a good stop when you need a present for someone.

Quick answers

Where is it?

1914 McCallie Ave, Chattanooga, TN 37404.

What are the hours?

The Trail lists Monday to Friday 11 to 5, Saturday 10 to 4 and Sunday 12 to 4.

Thrift tip: Weekday mornings are usually the calmest. Check the map for today's hours before you go.
