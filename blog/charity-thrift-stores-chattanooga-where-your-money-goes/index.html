💛 Charity Thrift Stores in Chattanooga: Where Your Money Goes

Guide October 18, 2027 · 2 min read

When you shop at a charity thrift store, your money does more than buy a lamp. It goes to work in the community. Many of the shops on the Chattanooga Thrift Trail are run by nonprofits, and each supports a specific cause.

Who benefits

  • Ladies of Charity Thrift Store: neighbors facing hardship, covering medicine, utilities, housing and clothing.
  • Providence Thrift Store: Providence Ministries, which fights homelessness, hunger and addiction.
  • SACs Thrift Avenue: the Sexual Assault Victims Advocacy Center.
  • Goodwill: job training, vocational skills and employment placement.
  • The Thrift Store (Chambliss Center): foster care and childcare programs for children.
  • Humane Educational Society Boutique & Thrift: animal sheltering and adoption.
  • Bethel Thrift Store: Bethel Bible Village, a children's home.
  • Northside Neighborhood House: neighbor-assistance programs.
  • Samaritan Center: emergency help for families in eastern Hamilton County.
  • Habitat for Humanity ReStore: affordable home construction.
  • Nifty Thrifty: the Chattanooga Rescue Mission.

How to find them

The Shops & Charities list shows a "Proceeds benefit" note under every shop that supports a cause. Pick a cause you care about and plan a loop around it.

Two ways to give

Shop with them and donate to them. Both help.

Why it matters

Thrift store sales often fund programs that otherwise rely on donations alone. Northside Neighborhood House, for example, reports that thrift store sales make up almost half of its total income. That means your purchase can matter more than you think.

Quick answers

How do I know where a shop's money goes?

Check the "Proceeds benefit" note on each shop in the Shops & Charities list.

Are all thrift stores charities?

No. Some are for-profit, so check each shop.

Thrift tip: Ask about donation hours before you drop off. They are not always the same as shopping hours.

Shops that are not on this list are regular for-profit businesses, as far as we could confirm. Plan a route on the Chattanooga thrift trail map.
