🧒 The Thrift Store Benefiting Chambliss Center: 150 Years of Caring for Kids

Charity November 1, 2027 · 2 min read

Two of Chattanooga's best-organized thrift stores share a mission that is older than almost anything else in the city. The Thrift Store, on Brainerd Rd and on Dayton Blvd in Red Bank, benefits the Chambliss Center for Children.

A very old nonprofit

The Chambliss Center began in 1872, when a group of Chattanooga women saw how many children needed a place to stay, rented a house and started serving local youth. In 2022 it celebrated 150 years of service.

What your purchase supports

  • Therapeutic foster care, which places children with trained foster families.
  • A Transitional Living Program, started in 2017, for young adults aging out of foster care.
  • Isaiah 117 House in Hamilton County, a calm, safe place for children while they wait for placement after being removed from unsafe homes.

Where to shop

  • 3723 Brainerd Rd in Brainerd: reviewers note a good plus-size and kids selection.
  • 1936 Dayton Blvd in Red Bank: reviewers praise the furniture and handmade items from local makers.

Each location shows a "Proceeds benefit" note in the Shops & Charities list.

Why local nonprofits depend on thrift

Unlike a large national charity, a local organization like the Chambliss Center serves children in the community around it. Thrift sales help fund its work year after year, which makes each purchase part of a long local story.

Quick answers

How many Thrift Store locations are there?

The Trail lists two: 3723 Brainerd Rd and 1936 Dayton Blvd in Red Bank.

Can I donate there?

Yes, but ask the staff about donation hours and accepted items first.

Thrift tip: Donating counts too. Ask the staff for current donation hours and what they are able to accept.
