📍 Thrifting in Brainerd and East Brainerd: A Neighborhood Guide

Neighborhood March 6, 2028 · 2 min read

Brainerd is a part of Chattanooga that rewards a thrift trip. Shops are spread along Brainerd Rd and nearby streets, with several more in East Brainerd, and a few stand out for the people behind them.

Brainerd

  • The Thrift Store, benefiting Chambliss Center (3723 Brainerd Rd): rotating inventory with a good plus-size and kids selection.
  • America's Thrift Stores (5844 Brainerd Rd): clean and well organized, though reviews note prices run higher than expected.
  • Pop's & Granny's Thrift Store (3238 Wilcox Blvd): a beloved neighborhood shop where reviewers highlight the warmth of the owners as much as the prices.

East Brainerd

  • Goodwill Store, E. Brainerd (7601 E Brainerd Rd): reviewers often name staff who went out of their way to help.
  • Hunts Thrift (1301 Hickory Valley Rd): reviews are mixed, so go for the chance of a good find.
  • Archive Thrift Store (6215 Lee Hwy): a thrift store in the Hamilton Place area.

Build a loop

Start on Brainerd Rd and work east. Save the longest stop for the end. See the Brainerd and East Brainerd and Hamilton Place shop lists, or build the route on the Chattanooga thrift trail map.

What makes the area good

Brainerd has a mix of charity stores, national chains and small neighborhood shops, which means variety in a short drive. The shops are spread out, so a car helps.

Quick answers

What time is best?

Mornings tend to be calmer. Check each shop's hours first, since they differ.

Which shops support charities?

The Thrift Store benefits Chambliss Center and Goodwill funds job training. See the Shops & Charities list.

Thrift tip: Small neighborhood shops often have shorter hours. Check the map before you go.
