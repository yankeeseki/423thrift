🌸 Spring Break Staycation: A Chattanooga Thrift Day for the Whole Family

Guide March 20, 2028 · 2 min read

Spring break does not need a plane ticket. A thrift day at home can be an adventure, especially with kids and teens. Turn it into a treasure hunt and the whole family gets involved.

Set the rules

  • Everyone gets a small budget, like $10.
  • Each person finds something useful, something funny and something beautiful.
  • Share your finds at the end of the day.

Plan the loop

Choose four or five stops and a lunch break in the middle. Open the Chattanooga thrift trail map, tap Let's Go Thrifting, and let it order the stops. For variety, mix a big thrift store, a vintage shop and an antique mall.

Keep kids engaged

  • Give each child a scavenger-hunt list.
  • Let teens choose a shop.
  • Bring snacks and water.
  • Take breaks. Antique malls are great, but not for everyone.

Good stops for families

The Thrift Store on Brainerd Rd is known for a good kids' selection. Vinterest in Hixson welcomes pets. Goodwill locations have large toy and book sections.

Keep a small prize

Give the winner of the family vote a small prize, like choosing a movie or picking dessert. It keeps the competition friendly.

Quick answers

What age is this good for?

Elementary-age kids through teens usually enjoy it. Younger children may need breaks.

How long should the day be?

Three to five hours with a lunch break.

Thrift tip: End the day by voting on the best find. The winner picks dinner.
