🎉 A Thrifty New Year: Declutter, Donate and Restock

Guide January 4, 2027 · 2 min read

January is the season of fresh starts, and thrift stores love it. Donation bins fill up and shelves refresh. If you want to get your home in order without buying a lot new, the thrift store is the best partner you have: a place to send what you no longer need and a place to find what you do.

Declutter in three piles

  • Keep: things you use or truly love.
  • Donate: clean, working items someone else will enjoy.
  • Toss: anything broken, stained or unsafe. Thrift stores pay to dispose of what they cannot sell, so please do not leave it at the door.

Donate where it does the most good

Many shops on the Trail are run by nonprofits, which means your donation funds local work. Check the "Proceeds benefit" notes in the Shops & Charities list to see where yours goes, from job training to foster care to animal sheltering.

Restock with intention

After a declutter, resist the urge to refill every shelf. Make a short list of what you actually need: a warm coat, a good lamp, a set of towels. Then hunt for exactly those things.

Make a one-in, one-out rule

The easiest way to keep a closet or shelf under control is to donate something every time you bring something home. It keeps the cycle going, helps local charities and stops thrift finds from piling up. Keep a donation bag near the door and drop it off on your next thrift trip.

Plan your donation drop

Pair your drop-off with a shopping loop. Donate at one shop, then browse a few more. Charity stores need clean, working, usable items. Ask what they accept, since each shop has different rules and hours. The Shops & Charities list shows who benefits from each one.

Quick answers

Should I donate before the new year for taxes?

Donations may be deductible, but rules vary. Ask the charity for a receipt and talk to a tax professional about your situation.

What sells best?

Clean clothing in good condition, kitchenware, books, small furniture and home decor are usually in demand.

Thrift tip: Donate first, shop second. Dropping off a bag and then browsing is a good way to keep your home from refilling.

Open the Trail, pick a day this month and make it a double-duty trip: donate on the way in, find something good on the way out.
