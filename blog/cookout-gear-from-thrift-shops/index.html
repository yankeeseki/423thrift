🍔 Cookout Gear From Thrift Shops: Grills, Coolers and Lawn Games

Seasonal July 2, 2029 · 3 min read

Fourth of July weekend is the biggest cookout of the summer, and a thrift loop can supply nearly everything you need for it.

Cooking and serving

  • Tongs, spatulas and forks with long handles.
  • Platters and big bowls for salads and sides.
  • Pitchers and drink dispensers. Check seals and spigots.
  • Cast iron skillets that work on a grill.

Cooling and carrying

Coolers are common, and a good one lasts for years. Check that the lid seals and the drain plug works, and wash the inside well. Baskets and wagons make it easier to carry plates and cups.

Sit and play

Folding chairs, camp stools, picnic tables and umbrellas turn a yard into a party. Lawn games such as cornhole, horseshoes and badminton sets turn up often. Count the pieces before you buy.

Safety first

Skip a used propane tank unless it is in date and inspected, and check any grill for rust, cracks and clean burners. Clean everything thoroughly before use.

Plan the loop

Go the weekend before. After the holiday, the cookout gear gets picked over quickly. Add a stop for ice and groceries to finish the trip.

Thrift tip: Bring a tape measure for folding tables and a few extra bags for small items.
