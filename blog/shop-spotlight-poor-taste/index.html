👗 Shop Spotlight: Poor Taste Downtown

Spotlight May 17, 2027 · 2 min read

Poor Taste is the kind of shop that makes you slow down. Tucked on East Main Street downtown, it is an owner-curated vintage clothing store, which means someone with a good eye has already done the hard work of sorting. What is on the rack is there on purpose.

What it is known for

Reviewers consistently describe the staff as approachable and knowledgeable, which matters when you are shopping vintage. Good staff can tell you about an era, fit, fabric and how to care for it. That makes it a great first stop if vintage clothing feels intimidating.

Why it is the Saturday featured shop

On the Thrift Trail, the featured shop is marked with a gold star. Saturdays belong to Poor Taste, a nice excuse to start a weekend loop downtown, then head to nearby North Shore and Southside shops.

Tips for shopping a curated vintage store

  • Go with an open mind. Vintage sizing is different from modern sizing, so try things on.
  • Check seams, underarms and hems. Ask the staff about any flaws.
  • Look for natural fabrics such as wool, cotton, silk and linen. They wear well and age gracefully.
  • Bring a friend with an honest opinion.

Poor Taste is at 61 E Main St, Chattanooga. Check the map for today's hours and add it to your route.

Why curated vintage is different

At a curated vintage shop, someone has already done the digging for you. Items are chosen, cleaned and organized, which saves time and helps you find well-made pieces. The tradeoff is price, since the owner pays for sourcing, rent and staff.

Quick answers

What does it sell?

Owner-curated vintage clothing, per reviewers and the Trail's listing.

Why is it the Saturday featured shop?

The Trail features a shop each day. Poor Taste takes the Saturday spotlight.
