🛍️ Last-Minute Thrifted Gifts Under $20

Seasonal December 21, 2026 · 2 min read

It is the week before Christmas, you are out of ideas and nearly out of time. A thrift run can save the day, and you can leave with something thoughtful for under $20. Here is a short list that works for almost anyone.

Ten ideas that work

  • A vintage mug or teacup with a bag of good tea or coffee tucked inside.
  • A hardcover book chosen for the person, in a nice dust jacket.
  • A cozy wool sweater or scarf in a classic pattern.
  • A small framed print or piece of local art.
  • A cast-iron skillet, seasoned and wrapped, for the person who cooks.
  • A set of cloth napkins, tied with twine.
  • A vintage board game or deck of cards for family night.
  • A little brass or glass candleholder with a fresh candle.
  • A plant in a thrifted planter.
  • A record, if you know what they already love.

How to shop fast

Pick two or three stops close together and let the Trail order them. Charity thrift stores are the quickest place to find mugs, books and kitchen items. If you have time for one curated stop, Edith's Emporium on McCallie Ave is known for gifts, home decor and locally made goods.

Wrap it like you meant it

The difference between a last-minute gift and a thoughtful one is often the wrapping. Use brown paper and twine, a fabric scarf or a thrifted basket. Tuck a sprig of rosemary or a candy cane under the ribbon. Add a short note about where you found the gift and why it made you think of the person.

Where to go when time is short

Pick the biggest store near you and spend 30 focused minutes. Start in housewares and books, then check jewelry and accessories. Use the Chattanooga thrift store map to see which shops are open today and which are closest.

Quick answers

What are the safest last-minute picks?

Books, mugs, candles in a thrifted holder, cloth napkins and picture frames are easy to find and hard to get wrong.

Can I wrap and give it the same day?

Yes. Wash glass and ceramics, wipe everything down and wrap right away.

Thrift tip: Keep a wrapped backup gift on hand. A thrifted candle and a bag of tea has saved plenty of last-minute invitations.

Check each shop's hours before you go. Holiday hours can change, and the shop's own website or social media is the best place to confirm.
