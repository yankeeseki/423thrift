🗺️ How the Chattanooga Thrift Trail Works: The Complete Guide

How-To October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Chattanooga Thrift Trail is a map and route planner for thrift stores, vintage shops and antique malls in Chattanooga, Tennessee and nearby North Georgia. There is nothing to download and no account to make. This guide walks through everything it can do, from your first tap to sharing a plan with a friend. For the short, dense reference version, see the full how-to guide.

Start with one tap

Tap Let's Go Thrifting. The Trail asks for your location so it can begin near you, then builds a driving loop of the shops that are open today and orders them around their hours. Closed shops are set aside automatically, so you will not drive to a dark storefront. After you start, one big button runs the day: tap Continue to the next shop whenever you finish one, and the Trail ticks the shop off and moves you on. A small line under it names your current stop, and double-tap the button to undo a stray tap, and press and hold it for directions to the shop shown. If you would rather not share your location, you can pick your own starting shop.

Choose the experience you want

Tap the small Filter link under Your Route. The panel has five boxes: Thrift shops, Curated shops, Antique malls, Lookout Loop and Scenic City Loop, the hand-picked favorites of the Trail's moderators. Every box you check adds its shops to your route, and no shop appears twice. Lookout Loop is checked to start. Uncheck them all and the map clears on purpose until you pick again or tap Reset.

You can also narrow by neighborhood, show only shops open today, only shops that close at 6 PM or later, only shops that support a charity, or only shops you have not visited yet.

Everything else waits in the second list

Whatever you check becomes your route, and every other shop drops into the list under it. In the classic view it is called More Shops. With any other mix it is called All Other Shops. Choose Antique malls only, for example, and your thrift stores and vintage shops wait there, one tap on the plus button away from joining the route.

Reading the map

  • Numbers show your shops in driving order. The gold star marks a Featured Shop and carries its shop number too.
  • Gold diamonds are bonus shops that are off the loop until you add them.
  • Names: tap or hover on a shop to see its short name, or switch on the Names button for all of them.
  • The map opens framed to fit every shop on your route and re-frames when you add or remove a shop.

Make the route yours

Remove a shop with the minus button, add one with the plus button, start somewhere else with the play button, or reorder with the arrows. The loop and the miles update right away. Learn the details in build your perfect thrift route.

Plan ahead, track progress and share

  • Plan ahead: pick a future date and the Trail builds the route using that day's hours.
  • The big Continue to button (or the Visited box on any card) crosses shops off as you go. Your progress is saved on your device. See saving your progress.
  • Share the Trail by Instagram, Facebook, X, text or a copied link, or open the Trail Poster.
  • Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. Here is how to do it.

Shops, charities and reviews

The Shops & Charities page lists every shop by neighborhood with addresses, hours and the cause each charity shop supports. Visitors can also leave reviews on each shop. Learn more in reviews and featured shops.

Updates arrive by themselves

When the Trail is updated, the page notices and quietly refreshes itself the next time you are away from it or idle, with no banner and nothing to tap. Your progress is saved first, so you do not lose anything.

Quick answers

Does the Thrift Trail cost anything?

Not right now. There is no account and nothing to download. The tip buttons at the bottom are optional.

How many shops are on it?

more than forty thrift stores, vintage shops and antique malls around Chattanooga and nearby North Georgia, including bonus shops you can add.

Does it show if a shop is open?

Yes. Each shop uses the shop's listed hours for the day, but always confirm before you drive because hours can change.

Do I need to refresh the page to see updates?

No. The Trail checks for new versions on its own and refreshes itself, keeping your saved progress.

Thrift tip: Open the Trail in the morning. Shops are freshest and you have time for a full loop.
