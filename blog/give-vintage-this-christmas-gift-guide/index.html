🎁 Give Vintage This Christmas: Why Gifts With a Past Are the Best Kind

Holiday December 7, 2026 · 2 min read

This Christmas, try giving something that already has a story. Vintage gifts feel different. They are specific, surprising and made to last, and shopping for them is half the fun.

Why vintage makes a great gift

  • It is one of a kind. Nobody else will give the same thing.
  • It has a story. A 1960s serving dish or a hand-knit scarf starts a conversation.
  • Quality lasts. Many older things were built better than their modern versions.
  • It fits a budget. You can give something meaningful for $10 to $40.
  • It is kinder to the planet. A reused gift is one fewer new thing made.
  • It supports local people. Your money goes to local small businesses and charities.

Gift ideas by person

  • The cook: cast iron, vintage mixing bowls, a recipe box with handwritten cards.
  • The reader: a first-printing paperback or a set of old hardcovers.
  • The music fan: a record from the year they were born.
  • The host: cut-glass tumblers, a cocktail set or a serving tray.
  • The collector: pins, postcards, Pyrex or vintage toys.
  • The one with everything: a framed vintage print or a brass lamp.

Where to shop

Vinterest Southside is open through December and has many vendors in one place, and Vinterest in Hixson stays open after. Antique malls like Dirty Jane's Antiques and Vintage & More On The Blvd in Red Bank, Poor Taste downtown and the East Ridge Antique District are all great for gifts.

Make it special

Clean the item, wrap it nicely and add a note on where you found it. Tell them why it made you think of them.

Set a vintage gift budget

Vintage gifting works at any budget. A few dollars buys a lovely brooch, a hardcover book or a pair of cloth napkins. For $25 to $40 you can find a nice serving platter, a leather bag or a cast iron skillet. Setting a limit before you go keeps the hunt fun and stops one pricey find from taking over your list.

Pair a find with something new

A vintage teacup looks even better with a tin of loose-leaf tea. An old cookbook is more fun with a wooden spoon. A used record pairs well with a gift card to a local coffee shop. Mixing old and new makes a gift feel complete.

Quick answers

Is it okay to give secondhand gifts?

More and more people think so, especially when the gift is clean, thoughtful and well wrapped. A gift with a story often means more than a store-bought one.

What if it is not perfect?

Check items for chips, stains and smells before buying, and keep your receipt. Antique malls and vendors have different return policies, so ask first.

Thrift tip: Shop the first two weeks of December while selection is still good, and ask the shop about gift wrapping or gift receipts.
